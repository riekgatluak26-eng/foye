Who We Are

A Youth-Led Movement for Change

Foundation for Youth Empowerment (FOYE) is a youth-led organization based in Yambio, South Sudan, committed to empowering young people through initiatives that address knowledge gaps, skills development, mentoring, youth unemployment, and economic self-reliance.

Mission: To empower youth with the skills, knowledge, and opportunities they need to become self-reliant and active contributors to society.

Vision: A South Sudan where every young person has the chance to reach their full potential.

Our Programs

Initiatives to empower youth and promote self-reliance.

  • Sanitary pad distribution
